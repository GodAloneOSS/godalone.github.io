GodAlone Open Source

Free and open-source software and resources created to benefit people, for the sake of Allah.

Everything here — apps, source code, Quran text, translations, books and audio — is free to use, copy, modify and share. No cost, no account, no catch. May it be a means for someone, somewhere, to draw closer to the Qur'an.
